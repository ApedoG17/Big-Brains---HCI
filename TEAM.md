# Team Agreement

## Communication Channels

- **Primary Channel:** WhatsApp Group
- **Emergency Channel:** Phone Calls
- **Expected Response Time:** Team members should respond to messages within 6 hours. Urgent messages should be acknowledged within 2 hours whenever possible.

---

## Meetings

- **Regular Meeting Day/Time:**
  - Monday: 5:00 PM – 9:00 PM (Online)
  - Thursday: 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM (Physical)

- **Meeting Platform/Location:**
  - Google Meet
  - University of Ghana Law School

- **Meeting Minutes Keeper:** Documentation and Evidence Lead

- **Attendance Rule:** Members should notify the team at least 12 hours before a meeting if they cannot attend. Missing two consecutive meetings without a valid reason may result in a formal warning.

---

## Work Division

Tasks will be assigned based on each member's skills, interests, and availability. All tasks will be tracked using GitHub Issues and the project board. Every completed task must be reviewed by at least one other team member before it is merged into the main branch. Team members are expected to provide regular progress updates during meetings.

---

## Version Control Rules

- Every feature must be developed on a separate feature branch.
- Branch names should follow formats such as:
  - `feature/login`
  - `feature/project-dashboard`
  - `feature/blueprint-upload`
  - `bugfix/upload-error`
- Commit messages should be clear and descriptive using prefixes such as:
  - `feat:`
  - `fix:`
  - `docs:`
  - `style:`
  - `refactor:`
- Every Pull Request must be reviewed and approved before merging.
- Direct commits to the `main` branch are not allowed.

---

## Academic Integrity and AI Rules

The team will use AI only as a support tool for brainstorming, research, documentation, debugging, and learning. Every member is responsible for understanding and explaining their own contributions. All AI usage will be recorded in the project's `AI_USAGE.md` file. Plagiarism, freeloading, dishonest AI use, and submitting work that cannot be explained are strictly prohibited.

---

## Conflict Resolution

If disagreements arise, the team will first discuss the issue respectfully and work together to reach a solution. Members who repeatedly miss meetings, fail to complete assigned tasks, or submit dishonest work will receive a formal warning. If the issue continues, it will be documented and reported to the course instructor. The team is committed to maintaining professionalism, accountability, and open communication throughout the project.
