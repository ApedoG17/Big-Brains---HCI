# 00 - Client Brief and Problem Statement

## 1. Project title

## 2. Client background

Who is the client? What do they do? Who are their customers/users?

## 3. Problem statement

Describe the real-world problem without jumping immediately to a technical solution.

## 4. Current process

How does the client currently solve this problem? Include manual steps, tools, spreadsheets, paper forms, WhatsApp workflows, etc.

## 5. Intended users

List each user group and explain what they need from the system.

## 6. Proposed solution summary

Give a short, non-technical description of the proposed software.

## 7. Client value

How will the solution help the client? Consider time, cost, accuracy, record keeping, service delivery, reporting, customer experience, or growth.

## 8. Constraints

List schedule, budget, internet access, hardware, client availability, legal/privacy, and data constraints.

## 9. Risks

What could go wrong? What is the fallback plan?

## 10. Evidence

Attach or link interview notes, meeting minutes, photos, screenshots, sketches, or client confirmation.
